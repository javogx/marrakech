Top 3 places to visit in

Marrakech

Jemaa el-Fnaa

Jemaa El-Fnaa is a famous, busy main square in Marrakech, Morocco. During the day, you can see street performers, orange juice stands, and market stalls. At night, it turns into a big open-air food market with glowing lights, delicious food smoke, and music playing in the background near the famous Koutoubia Mosque.

Jardin Majorelle

Jardin Majorelle is a famous botanical garden and museum in Marrakech, Morocco. Created by the French painter Jacques Majorelle in the 1920s and later saved by fashion designer Yves Saint Laurent, it features bright cobalt blue buildings—known as "Majorelle Blue"—surrounded by exotic plants, giant cacti, quiet ponds, and shady paths.

Madrasa Ben Youssef

Ben Youssef Madrasa is a historical Islamic college in Marrakech, Morocco, and one of the largest madrasas in North Africa. Founded in the 14th century and later expanded by the Saadian dynasty, it served for centuries as a center for higher religious learning. The complex is world-famous for its striking Moroccan-Andalusian architecture, featuring a grand central courtyard with a pool, intricate zellij tilework, carved cedar wood, and detailed stucco work.
